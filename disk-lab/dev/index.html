        What’s new, version by version更新履歴(版ごとの変更)

        An interactive, physics-based visualization of protoplanetary disks, from magnetic fields and disk winds to planet formation and observational signatures.磁場と円盤風から、惑星の形成、観測で見える姿まで。原始惑星系円盤を物理にもとづいて描き、操作しながら見られるようにしたものです。

        The model is schematic: the equations and solutions come from the literature; strengths, colours and other settings are chosen so that the structure reads clearly. It is not the output of a simulation.ただし模式的なモデルです。式と解は文献のものを使い、強さや色などの値は構造が読み取りやすいように選んでいます。シミュレーションの結果そのものではありません。

        It is drawn on your GPU: along each line of sight the light emitted and absorbed is added up (volume rendering). Field lines and annotations are drawn over it as vector lines. Made by Shoji Mori.ブラウザーの GPU で、視線に沿って光の放射と吸収を足し合わせて描いています(ボリュームレンダリング)。磁力線と注釈はベクトルの線で重ねています。作成:森 昇志。

        What is in it入っているもの

        Disk円盤
        Its characteristic radius is 30 au: the surface density falls as 1/R and, beyond about 30 au, steeply, as a Gaussian (Σ ∝ R−1 e−(R/30 au)²), so the gas thins out past it rather than ending there. The thickness, H/R = 0.045 (R/1 au)1/4, flares outward. It is drawn about 1.7 times thicker than its own temperature would make it (about 174 K at 1 au, which holds up H/R ≈ 0.026), so that its layers show; the surface density is kept, and everything built on the thickness follows it (the lit surface, the wind’s base, the gaps). The star has the Sun’s mass and 2.7 times its luminosity (a young star); the disk feeds it 10−7 solar masses a year. These are the defaults: under Model the star’s luminosity, the accretion rate, the turbulence, the lever arm of the wind and more can be changed, and the temperature, the edge of the thermally ionized inner disk and the wind follow (the numbers that follow are given there).特性半径は 30 au。面密度は半径に反比例し、30 au あたりから外ではガウス関数の形で急に減ります(Σ ∝ R−1 e−(R/30 au)²)。円盤はそこで切れず、外へ薄れていきます。厚み H/R = 0.045 (R/1 au)1/4 で外ほど反り返ります。層の構造が見えるよう、自身の温度(1 au で約 174 K)が支える厚み(H/R ≈ 0.026)の 1.7 倍ほどに描いています。面密度は変えず、厚みで決まるもの(照らされた表層、風の根元、ギャップ)はそれに従います。中心星は太陽の質量で、明るさは太陽の 2.7 倍(若い星)。円盤から星へ年に太陽質量の 10−7 倍のガスが降着します。ここに書いた数は既定の値です。「モデル」で星の光度、降着率、乱流の強さ、風のレバーアームなどを変えると、温度、熱電離した内側円盤の縁、円盤風がそれに従います(その数も「モデル」に出ます)。
        Gas and its temperatureガスと温度
        The gas carries small dust grains, well mixed with it (they evaporate inside about 0.08 au); the two are shown together, as the gas. The temperature follows from the starlight and the accretion (Chiang & Goldreich 1997; Mori et al. 2019, 2021, 2025): the grains in the thin layer a few scale heights up that absorbs the grazing starlight (the white line on the cut, τ* = 1) are superheated, about 590 K at 1 au, and the interior below is warmed by the light they give off again, about 155 K at 1 au. In a magnetically accreting disk the accretion releases its heat high up, where most of it escapes; at 10−7 solar masses a year it still warms the interior to about 174 K at 1 au, so the water snow line lies near 1.1 au. Only inside about 0.7 au, where the disk is turbulent, does it make the midplane the hottest, above 1000 K (up to about 1500 K, where the dust evaporates). The temperature shows on the cut (its Temperature quantity) and in the observed looks: in the visible and mid-infrared looks the lit surface is what you see (its light is integrated analytically).ガスには小さな塵がよく混ざっています(約 0.08 au より内側では蒸発してありません)。この二つを合わせて「ガス」として見せています。温度は星の光と降着から求めています(Chiang & Goldreich 1997、Mori et al. 2019, 2021, 2025)。斜めに当たる星の光を吸う数スケールハイト上の薄い層(断面の白い線、τ* = 1)の塵は過熱されて 1 au で約 590 K、その下の内部は塵が出し直す光で温まり 1 au で約 155 K です。磁場で降着する円盤では降着の熱が上の方で出て大半が逃げますが、年に太陽質量の 10−7 倍の降着では内部も 1 au で約 174 K まで温まり、水のスノーラインは約 1.1 au にあります。乱流のある約 0.7 au より内側でだけ、降着の熱で赤道面が最も熱くなり、1000 K を超えます(塵が蒸発する約 1500 K まで)。温度は断面の「温度」と観測の見え方で見られます。可視光と中間赤外の見え方では、この照らされた表層が見えます(光り方は解析的に積分しています)。
        Pebbles and the snow line小石とスノーライン
        Larger dust settles to the midplane and is coated in ice where it is colder than 160 K, beyond about 1 au. The pebbles’ disk ends at 20 au, two thirds of the gas’s: millimetre dust disks are smaller than their gas disks (by a factor of about 1.5 to 3 in Lupus; Ansdell et al. 2018). They gather a little just outside the snow line.大きめの塵は赤道面に沈み、160 K より冷たい所(約 1 au より外)で氷をまといます。小石の円盤はガスの 3 分の 2、20 au で終わります(ミリ波の塵の円盤はガスの円盤より小さく、おおかみ座の円盤で 1.5〜3 分の 1。Ansdell et al. 2018)。小石はスノーラインのすぐ外に少したまります。
        Field and wind磁場と円盤風
        Self-similar solutions for a cold magnetocentrifugal wind of the Blandford & Payne (1982) kind, each line with the lever arm λ of its foot. λ is 4 at the dust’s inner edge; by default it falls to 1.2 at the edge of the thermally ionized inner disk (a heavily loaded wind, as one helped by the gas’s pressure can be) and rises to 5 at 30 au, the disk’s characteristic radius: the inner wind dense and the outer one light (simulations give λ of about 1.1 to 5, with no clear trend with radius). The lines take the cold solutions for λ of 1.8 to 4; the mass budget takes λ itself: the accretion rate grows outward by what the wind carries away, so that mass and angular momentum are both kept, and about a third of the gas reaching the thermally ionized inner disk goes on to the star. By default the wind is symmetric; as an option it is stronger on one side, as for a field aligned with the rotation (Mori, Bai & Tomida 2025).Blandford & Payne (1982) 型の、冷たい磁気遠心風の自己相似解。磁力線ごとに、足元の半径に応じたレバーアーム λ の解を使います。λ は塵の内縁で 4。既定では熱電離した内側円盤の縁で 1.2 まで下がり(ガスの圧力が打ち上げを助ける風のように、質量を多く積んだ風)、円盤の特徴半径 30 au で 5 まで上がります。内側の風は濃く、外側は軽くなります(シミュレーションの λ は約 1.1〜5 で、半径による系統的な違いははっきりしません)。磁力線の形には λ = 1.8〜4 の冷たい解を使い、質量の勘定には λ そのものを使います。風が運び去る分だけ降着率は外へ増え、質量と角運動量の両方が保たれます。熱電離した内側円盤に届いたガスの約 3 分の 1 が星まで届きます。既定では上下対称の風です。選ぶと、片側が強い風になります(磁場が回転と同じ向きの場合。Mori, Bai & Tomida 2025)。
        Jetジェット
        A narrow, fast flow along the axis, both ways, launched close to the star at the disk’s inner edge: 250 km/s, narrow (within 2 degrees from about 2 au up: about 1 au across at 10 au, 7 au at 100 au; narrower than measured jets, a choice of the picture), with knots, the shocks of a launch speed that varies (every 2.5 and 0.6 years), moving out with the gas (as measured in T Tauri jets: Frank et al. 2014; Ray & Ferreira 2021; Hartigan & Morse 2007; Agra-Amboage et al. 2011). With the one-sided option its lower lobe is 1.5 times slower and denser, as in some jets. It shows in the model look (pale yellow) and faintly in the visible one (its emission lines); not in the infrared, millimetre or gas-line looks.軸に沿って上下へ細く速く吹き出す流れ。星の近く、円盤の内縁から出ます。速さ 250 km/s、細く絞られています(約 2 au から上は 2° の中に収まり、幅は 10 au で約 1 au、100 au で 7 au。実際のジェットの測定より細く、絵の上の選択です)。吹き出す速さが揺らぐため衝撃波の結び目ができ(2.5 年ごとと 0.6 年ごと)、ガスとともに外へ動きます(T タウリ型星のジェットの観測から:Frank et al. 2014、Ray & Ferreira 2021、Hartigan & Morse 2007、Agra-Amboage et al. 2011)。上下非対称の選択肢では、下側が 1.5 倍遅く、そのぶん濃くなります(いくつかのジェットで見られるように)。モデルの見え方(淡い黄色)と、可視光でかすかに(輝線の光)見えます。赤外線、ミリ波、ガス輝線では見えません。
        Field and gas磁場とガスの結びつき
        The gas is weakly ionized. Near the midplane, Ohmic diffusion leaves a dead zone (out to about 7 au) where the field cannot act. Just above it, where the gas begins to couple, the field lines bend and a thin accretion layer carries the gas inward (Bai & Stone 2013; Mori, Bai & Tomida 2025); above that the field opens and the gas is lifted slowly (the wind base), until it leaves as the wind from the irradiation surface, a few scale heights up. Beyond the dead zone the accretion layer lies at the midplane. Inside about 0.7 au the gas is ionized by heat and turbulent (where the turbulence’s own accretion heating keeps it above about 1000 K); beyond about 10 au, where the whole column is ionized, weakly turbulent. Everything else, the dead zone, the accretion layer and the wind included, is laminar: not turbulent. With the field aligned with the rotation (the one-sided option), the Hall effect pushes the accretion layer up to the lower side’s surface, out to about 10 au.ガスの電離は弱く、赤道面付近はオーム散逸で磁場が効かないデッドゾーン(約 7 au まで)。そのすぐ上、ガスが磁場と結びつき始める所で磁力線が折れ曲がり、薄い降着層がガスを内側へ運びます(Bai & Stone 2013、Mori, Bai & Tomida 2025)。その上では磁力線が開いてガスがゆっくり持ち上がり(風の根元)、数スケールハイト上の照射面から円盤風として吹き出します。デッドゾーンより外では、降着層は赤道面にあります。約 0.7 au より内側は熱で電離して乱流になり(乱流による降着の加熱で約 1000 K を超える範囲)、約 10 au より外側は柱全体が電離して弱い乱流があります。それ以外は、デッドゾーンも降着層も円盤風も、乱流のない層流です。磁場が回転と同じ向きのとき(上下非対称の選択肢)は、ホール効果で降着層が下側の表面まで押し上げられます(約 10 au まで)。
        Planet惑星
        Up to four planets, from 0.05 au (where the star’s field cuts off the disk) to 20 au, of 10 Earth masses to 3 Jupiter masses (heavier gaps turn eccentric, which this model does not show); the first a Jupiter at 10 au. Each opens a gap (the profile of Kanagawa et al. 2017 for a viscosity α = 10−3 by default: a Jupiter at 3 au leaves it nearly empty from 2.6 to 3.4 au, its walls reaching 1.5 and 4.5 au), raises spiral waves (their decay from the global solution of Rafikov 2002, their strength growing with the mass up to a few thermal masses), and, above the pebble isolation mass (Bitsch et al. 2018; 43 Earth masses at 3 au), traps pebbles in a ring at the pressure maximum just outside. Neighbours keep at least four mutual Hill radii apart. Each has a disk of its own out to 0.4 Hill radii that glows, so that it shows from afar as a point of warm light; the disk flares, from H/r of about 0.1 near the planet to 0.3 at its edge for a Jupiter at 3 au (its outer part takes the temperature of the gas around it), and a planet of only a few thermal masses wears a nearly round envelope instead. Closer in than about 0.3 au the gap’s profile is extrapolated beyond the disks it was fitted to.惑星を 4 つまで、0.05 au(星の磁場が円盤を切り取る所)から 20 au に、地球の 10 倍から木星の 3 倍の質量で(それより重いとギャップが楕円になり、このモデルでは描けません)。最初の 1 つは 10 au の木星。惑星はギャップを開け(Kanagawa et al. 2017 の形。既定の粘性 α = 10−3 では、3 au の木星なら 2.6〜3.4 au はほぼ空で、壁は 1.5 au と 4.5 au まで)、渦巻きの波を立て(減衰は Rafikov 2002 の大域解、強さは熱的質量の数倍まで質量とともに増える)、小石の孤立質量(Bitsch et al. 2018。3 au で地球の 43 倍)を超えると、すぐ外の圧力の山に小石の環を作ります。隣どうしは相互ヒル半径の 4 倍以上離します。ヒル半径の 0.4 倍まで広がる周惑星円盤が光り、遠くからも暖かい光の点として見えます。周惑星円盤は外ほど厚く反り返り(3 au の木星で H/r が惑星のそばの約 0.1 から縁の 0.3 へ。外側はまわりのガスの温度になるため)、熱的質量の数倍しかない惑星では、円盤ではなく丸い包みをまといます。約 0.3 au より内側では、ギャップの形は式が確かめられた円盤の外へ延ばして使っています。
        The star’s magnetosphere星の磁気圏
        The star’s dipole field truncates the disk at 0.05 au; inside, gas falls onto the star along closed field lines.星の双極子磁場が 0.05 au で円盤を切り取り、その内側ではガスが閉じた磁力線に沿って星へ落ちます。
        Envelopeエンベロープ
        The rest of the parent cloud, falling in as it rotates (the solution of Ulrich 1976, its centrifugal radius at 30 au, the disk’s characteristic radius, within which it lands), with a cavity cleared along the axis. At a given distance it is densest near the midplane, but there it lies in the disk’s shadow: what shines is the lit walls of the cavity (the starlight it scatters). In the model look it also glows faintly of itself, with its density, so that its midplane shows too. A part turned off under Show neither shines nor shades the others: with the gas off, the disk’s shadow goes.回転しながら落ちる母体の雲の名残(Ulrich 1976 の解。遠心力半径を円盤の特徴半径 30 au にとり、その内側に着地)。軸に沿って空洞が開いています。同じ距離では赤道面の近くで最も濃くなりますが、そこは円盤の影で星の光が届かず、明るく見えるのは光の当たる空洞の壁です(散らされた星の光)。モデルの見え方では、密度に応じて自らもかすかに光るので、赤道面も見えます。「見せるもの」で消したものは光らず、ほかのものに影も落としません。ガスを消すと円盤の影もなくなります。

        The looks見え方

        Modelモデル
        The model’s own quantities, only what is there: colour is the material, each with its own (by default the gas and its dust white, the pebbles dark grey where rock and grey where icy, the wind pale blue, the jet pale yellow, the envelope lavender, the planets and their disks gold, the star’s magnetosphere violet and its accretion curtains pink; the key in a corner of the picture), brightness how much of it is there (the gas glows in proportion to its density). The colours are chosen to be told apart and to look well together, not to show a temperature; those of the gas, the pebbles, the wind, the jet and the envelope, and that of the field lines drawn over the picture, can be changed with the swatch beside each in Show. The star lights the disk’s flared surface, its small grains scattering the light forward, so the near side is brighter; where its light does not reach, the midplane and the far side, the glow is lower. The faint turbulent pattern in the disk turns with it, faster inside; it is stronger where the gas is stirred (inside about 0.7 au, beyond about 10 au) and weak in the dead zone (as 1 : 0.12 : 0.3; e.g. Okuzumi & Hirose 2011), and it changes with height too, in eddies about a scale height across: quiet at the midplane and in the dead zone, stronger above them. At the higher quality levels the bright parts glow a little and what lies far behind is a little fainter, a choice of the picture. The gas’s own glow is drawn as a translucent layer that shows where the gas is, down to the midplane; the starlight its small grains scatter is dimmed on its way by the same grains as much as in visible light, so the lit surface behind the disk is hidden (a real disk is opaque in visible light). Under Look, its Light can be switched to “Own emission”: no light of a surface lit by the star; the gas and its dust, drawn as a volume, glow by density × temperature × opacity and absorb at the same grey opacity κ (2 cm²/g of gas by default, a slider from 0.001 to 100). Their temperature is a mock structure: down to a column Σ_s from above (0.1 g/cm² by default, a slider from 0.001 to 10), where the starlight reaches it, the surface is at the temperature of the grains the starlight heats directly; deeper, and in the shadow the inner disk casts on the outer tail, at the interior’s (the cut’s Temperature, with the optical depth toward the star replaced by the column from above over Σ_s, or kept where it is the larger: the shadow).         Limits限界

        • Compared with RADMC-3D for the same inputs, the millimetre look agrees; the gas line, the mid-infrared and the visible (scattered) light keep differences from the approximations used here.同じ入力の RADMC-3D の計算と比べると、ミリ波は一致し、ガス輝線、中間赤外、可視光(散乱光)には近似による差が残ります。
        • Time is compressed, and follows the view: at ×1 an orbit at 1 au takes 12 seconds while the whole disk is seen from 90 au or farther, and closer in the clock slows with the orbital period at the radius in view (to about 1/30 at the snow line, 1/15000 next to the star). An outburst runs on its own clock and fades in about half a minute (real ones last decades); a planet grows in seconds (really hundreds of thousands of years or more).時間は縮め、見ている範囲に合わせています。×1 では、円盤の全体を 90 au 以上離れて見るとき 1 au の公転が 12 秒で、寄るほど見ている半径の公転周期に比例して遅くなります(スノーラインで約 1/30、星のそばで約 1/15000)。増光は別の時計で 30 秒ほどで収まり(実際は数十年)、惑星は数秒で育ちます(実際は数十万年以上)。
        • Scattering is single, with an approximate allowance for light that seeps below the surface.散乱は一回だけで、表層の下へしみ込む光を近似で加えています。
